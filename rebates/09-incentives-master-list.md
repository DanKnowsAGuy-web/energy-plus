# 09. Incentives Master List (Federal, State, Utility)

Verified September 9, 2026. Post OBBBA (July 4, 2025) status throughout.

## Question

Build a reference of federal, state, and utility incentives for commercial HVAC, refrigeration, power quality, battery storage, solar PV, solar thermal, lighting, motors/VFDs, rate programs, and C-PACE financing, to drive a savings calculator that shows conditional bullet incentives by state, facility type, and measure (no dollar math).

## State programs by state

**Florida.** No state solar tax credit or rebate. C-PACE available but administered county by county; check local administrator before quoting. No confirmed state sales tax exemption for commercial solar equipment. Source: [Florida PACE, DSIRE](https://programs.dsireusa.org/system/program/detail/3869/florida-pace-programs).

**Texas.** No state income tax and no statewide solar rebate, but TX-PACE is active statewide via multiple local administrators (Texas PACE Authority, Lone Star PACE, others) for commercial retrofits, new construction, and renewables. Treat as "check administrator by jurisdiction." Source: [Texas Comptroller PACE](https://comptroller.texas.gov/programs/seco/funding/pace.php), [Texas PACE Authority](https://www.texaspaceauthority.org/property-assessed-clean-energy/).

**South Carolina.** State solar income tax credit equal to 25% of system cost, capped at $3,500/year (or 50% of tax liability), carries forward 10 years, no stated expiration. Applies to commercial solar. Confirm current cap with SC Department of Revenue. Source: [DSIRE](https://www.dsireusa.org/), [This Old House SC guide](https://www.thisoldhouse.com/solar-alternative-energy/solar-incentives-south-carolina).

**Georgia.** No state solar tax credit or rebate. C-PACE authorized and available through local administrators. Federal REAP is the main non-utility lever for rural small businesses. Source: [SEIA Georgia](https://seia.org/state-solar-policy/georgia-solar/).

**North Carolina.** No current state solar tax credit (prior 35% credit expired years ago). C-PACE authorized and operating through local administrators. Source: [Commercial-Solar.org NC guide](https://commercial-solar.org/north-carolina/solar-incentives/).

**California.** No state income tax credit for commercial solar, but an active property tax exclusion applies to qualifying new solar systems, and SGIP supports battery storage. SGIP's General Market, Equity, and Equity Resiliency budgets are reported closed to new commercial applicants as of 2026; only the RSSE AB 209 budget (income-qualified/resiliency focus) is confirmed open. Treat commercial SGIP as "check current budget status." Source: [FranklinWH SGIP 2026 guide](https://www.franklinwh.com/blog/a-complete-guide-to-california-sgip-program-in-2026).

**New York.** NY-Sun (NYSERDA) offers a per-watt upfront rebate for commercial/industrial solar PV via declining Megawatt Blocks that vary by region and change quarterly; check the NYSERDA tracker at quote time. No confirmed standalone statewide commercial battery rebate as of 2026; commercial storage economics rest on ConEd programs plus federal 48E and bonus depreciation. Source: [NYSERDA NY-Sun](https://www.nyserda.ny.gov/All-Programs/NY-Sun), [DSIRE NY-Sun C&I listing](https://programs.dsireusa.org/system/program/detail/22562/ny-sun-pv-incentive-program-commercial-and-industrial).

**Other states.** Look up DSIRE at dsireusa.org, filter by state and "Commercial" sector, and cross check the state energy office and the relevant utility's business rebate page directly, since DSIRE can lag.

## Utility programs by utility

- **FPL (Florida).** Business Lighting Program (per-lamp/fixture LED rebates), Heating and Cooling program with a VFD rebate, custom incentives for HVAC, envelope, water heating, refrigeration. [fpl.com/business/save](https://www.fpl.com/business/save/programs.html)
- **Duke Energy Florida.** Smart $aver Business Rebates: prescriptive HVAC, chillers, fans, refrigeration, kitchen equipment, envelope, plus custom incentives (pre-approval required). [duke-energy.com](https://www.duke-energy.com/business/products/smartsaver)
- **TECO (Tampa Electric).** Lighting (LED, controls, delamping) and HVAC (cooling replacement, controls, heat pumps); rebate paid within about six weeks of approval. [tecopartners.com](https://www.tecopartners.com/rebateprocessing/about/)
- **Oncor (Texas).** "Take A Load Off, Texas" annual program, roughly $8.5M earmarked for commercial, covering HVAC, lighting, weatherization, solar, load management; first-come first-served, resets yearly. [oncor.com](https://www.oncor.com/content/oncorwww/wire/en/home/energy-efficiency/smart-money--energy-incentives-cut-costs---bills.html)
- **CenterPoint Energy (Texas).** Commercial Electric Service Rebates for prescriptive equipment; C&I Standard Offer Program pays $175/kW demand reduction plus $0.06/kWh saved; separate solar rebate budget (about $1.5M for 2026), first-come first-served. [centerpointenergy.com](https://www.centerpointenergy.com/en-us/business/save-energy-money/efficiency-programs-rebates/electric-service-rebates)
- **AEP Texas.** SMART Source solar PV: $0.50/W up to 25 kW (max $1,250) or $0.25/W up to 200 kW (max $50,000); must complete within four months of pre-approval. [txreincentives.com](https://www.txreincentives.com/)
- **Texas demand response (ERCOT, not one utility).** Deregulated market: DR for large C&I load runs through ERCOT (Emergency Response Service, Responsive Reserve, ECRS) via aggregators, not the wires utility. Flag as "ERCOT market program, needs curtailable load and a qualified aggregator." [ERCOT ERS overview](https://cpowerenergy.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/12/ERCOT_ERS_LR_Web_Download_4.pdf)
- **Dominion Energy South Carolina.** Small Business Energy Solutions covers up to 90% of lighting/HVAC/water heating cost (cap $7,000) for accounts under 350,000 kWh/year, 5 or fewer accounts; confirm current bonus window. [dominionenergy.com](https://www.dominionenergy.com/south-carolina/save-energy/small-business-incentives)
- **Duke Energy Carolinas / Progress (NC, SC).** Smart $aver Business Rebates, same structure as Duke Florida; DEC (Piedmont) and DEP (eastern NC/parts of SC) are separate territories with differing tiers. [duke-energy.com](https://www.duke-energy.com/business/products/smartsaver)
- **Georgia Power.** Commercial Energy Efficiency Program: rebates for lighting, no-sweat doors, VFDs (up to $50/hp), custom measures (up to $75,000/building/year at $0.10/kWh saved); seasonal bonus periods, confirm current window. Administered by ICF. [georgiapower.com](https://www.georgiapower.com/business/save-money-and-energy/commercial-rebates-and-incentives.html)
- **PG&E (California).** Rebates for HVAC, lighting, motors, refrigeration, food service, water pumps; 0% financing over $5,000; demand response pays for peak curtailment, plus $0.06/kWh with Automated Demand Response. [pge.com](https://www.pge.com/en/save-energy-and-money/rebates-and-incentives/business-energy-efficiency-rebates.html)
- **SCE.** Commercial Energy Efficiency Program (CEEP), implemented by Willdan, covers efficiency and electrification; also runs demand response and EV charger commercial rebates. [sce.com](https://www.sce.com/save-money/rebates-financial-assistance/rebates-sce-marketplace)
- **Con Edison (New York).** C&I Energy Efficiency and Electrification Program for accounts averaging over 100 kW peak demand; up to 50% of cost for HVAC, building automation, refrigeration, insulation, fuel-switching (up to $70/MMBtu saved); separate Dynamic Load Management and Demand Response (Rider T); Southeast Queens Neighborhood Program adds bonuses. [coned.com](https://www.coned.com/-/media/files/coned/documents/save-energy-money/rebates-incentives-tax-credits/rebates-incentives-tax-credits-for-commercial-industrial-buildings-customers/commercial-and-industrial-program/program-manual.pdf)

## Confidence

High: federal 48E and bonus depreciation status, 179D termination date, Dominion SC, PG&E, ConEd, FPL, and Duke Energy program structures, all confirmed on primary program pages dated 2026. Medium: dollar/percentage details on utility rebates and state financing programs that shift by budget cycle or region, SGIP commercial availability, NY-Sun block rates. Low: exact C-PACE jurisdictional coverage in GA and NC, which varies city by city and needs a live administrator check before quoting a specific address.

## Unverified items

- EPA/DOE discretionary grants: legal status is actively litigated as of August 2026 and changes weekly. Do not trigger on any specific EPA or DOE grant without a per-program recheck at quote time.
- Florida statewide sales tax exemption for commercial solar/storage equipment was not confirmed; check Florida Department of Revenue directly.
- Current SGIP commercial budget status beyond RSSE AB 209 should be reconfirmed at the CPUC SGIP dashboard before display, since budgets close and reopen.
- Power factor and interruptible rate tariffs (as opposed to rebate programs) were not individually verified per utility; pull each commercial tariff schedule separately if the calculator triggers on rate structure.
- Georgia and North Carolina C-PACE coverage by county/city was not enumerated; treat as "ask local administrator" until a jurisdiction-level list is built.
