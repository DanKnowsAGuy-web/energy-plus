Method. Rebate score = 25% deemed-measure availability for our stack + 25% incentive generosity + 20% admin ease + 15% speed to payment + 15% program stability, each scored 0 to 5 from primary sources and scaled to 100. Peak-demand readiness is derived here from the September layer: strong when a program is confirmed with a published payment or the row is high confidence, available when the state sits in an organized wholesale market or a named utility program exists at medium confidence, thin when programs are known but unverified, none when no program was found. Program years roll over and funds exhaust; re-verify tier-1 states each program year and before any proposal cites a number older than six months.
