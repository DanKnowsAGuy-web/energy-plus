# Partner Program Entry Points: Energy Plus Ally Map

Research pass on published partner/channel/trade-ally/supplier programs for 20 target companies plus 4 adjacent capital/downstream players. Purpose: identify where Energy Plus (SC-based vendor-neutral integrator, HVAC compressor-level measures plus power quality/harmonics and transformer sourcing) can enter as a measure supplier or referral partner rather than compete head-on.

All URLs accessed 2026-09-27 unless a specific publish date is cited. Where a company publishes no revenue share, referral fee, exclusivity, or certification terms, that is noted as "none found" rather than guessed.

---

## Executive summary

1. Real, open, self-serve partner programs a small firm can join today: GridPoint (Reseller + Referral Program, application form), Turntide (Channel Partner Program with Partner Portal, VAR/ESCO/distributor tiers), Honeywell Building Solutions (Catalyst Partner Program, tiered, digital PRM platform), Johnson Controls OpenBlue (Partner Program + Partner Extensions, application via contact form), Verdigris (Channel Partner Program, now run by EnergyHUB 360 as outsourced partner manager).
2. Programs that exist and are open but are utility-program-specific, not company-level: CLEAResult (ATLAS Partner Hub is a trade-ally portal per utility program, plus a separate "Manufacturing Partners" invite), Willdan (trade ally registration lives inside each utility contract Willdan administers, not a Willdan-branded program).
3. Real programs, but structured as OEM/technology-integration deals rather than an open application: Encycle (OEM integration partnerships: Honeywell, Resideo, Carrier, Copeland, Distech, plus a named channel sales VP), BrainBox AI (three OEM partners: Trane, ABB, KMC Controls, plus AWS Partner Network), 75F/Carrier (75F Certified Partner program continues post-acquisition, feeds into Carrier Abound).
4. Bespoke business-development approach required (no published self-serve path, relationship-driven, deal-by-deal): Budderfly (grows technology stack by acquisition, e.g. Sunverge, not by onboarding outside vendors), Trane Technologies core channel (Comfort Specialist / Elite Dealer are HVAC dealer programs, not measure-supplier programs; Preferred Supplier Program is for Trane's own component supply chain, not a channel), Voltus (DER/OEM partnerships exist, but no public application path, all outbound), CPower/NRG (broker-partner relationships and OEM deals like Generac exist but are negotiated, not applied for), Carrier Abound (partner network exists but page had no public application content), Enel X North America (VPP Connect API plus named bilateral tech deals, e.g. Honeywell, Phoenix Energy; enterprise BD process), Sparkfund (vendor/OEM relationships are curated by Sparkfund's own team, not open applications), Ameresco (subcontractor-only, via COMPASS prequalification portal, project-by-project, not a measure-supplier channel), Redaptive (Integrated Partner Network is named but the join process is a contact form, effectively BD-led), Verdigris' underlying tech relationship, Centinel/Carbon Lighthouse (no partner program at all, in-house sensor/measure model), Metrus Energy (works through ESCO partners on a deal basis, not an open program), PowerSecure (contact-us only, no published program), Scale Microgrids (vendor/EPC/OEM financing relationships are BD-led), Nuveen Green Capital (C-PACE deal-by-deal with developers and contractors, no published contractor network), HASI (works through ESCOs on a case basis, no published developer-partner program).
5. No partner path found at all, despite searching: Budderfly (no vendor onboarding page of any kind), Centinel/Carbon Lighthouse (no partner program page), PowerSecure (no partner program page, "contact us" only), Nuveen Green Capital (no developer/contractor network page).
6. Highest-leverage near-term targets for Energy Plus given its compressor-cycling optimizer, coil restoration, and solar thermal assist: Encycle (an OEM-style software/hardware combination filing is closest analog to how Energy Plus's optimizer could sit inside a partner's stack), Turntide (open channel program, motor/electrification adjacency), Honeywell Building Solutions and Johnson Controls OpenBlue (both have real, structured, documented partner tiers open to system integrators, which is the closest legal category to Energy Plus).
7. CLEAResult and Willdan are best understood as implementers of utility rebate programs, not brand owners of a national partner network; entry is through the specific utility contract Willdan or CLEAResult is running in South Carolina/target territory, and through their manufacturer/trade-ally intake forms.
8. Financial/capital players (Nuveen Green Capital, HASI) do not have contractor or measure-supplier "programs" in the way a technology company does; they underwrite deals originated by developers, ESCOs, and contractors, so the entry point is bringing them a bankable project, not applying to a program.
9. Downstream generation/storage players (PowerSecure, Scale Microgrids) are candidates for referral relationships once Energy Plus identifies a customer with a genuine microgrid/storage need, but neither publishes a self-serve partner form; both are "contact us."
10. Overall picture: the biggest players with real digital partner portals (JCI, Honeywell, GridPoint, Turntide) are also the most crowded and hardware-centric; the companies whose business model most resembles Energy Plus's compressor-level measure supplier pitch (Encycle, BrainBox AI, Verdigris) reach the market through named OEM deals, so Energy Plus's best entry there is a direct pitch to their business-development contact, not a portal application.

- Public pages: https://turntide.com/new-partners/, https://turntide.com/about-us/turntide-turnkey-solutions/, launch release https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2022/12/09/2571071/0/en/Turntide-Technologies-Launches-Global-Channel-Partner-Program-With-Partners-Across-Three-Continents.html (Dec 9, 2022).

- Published terms: none found for specific revenue share or referral fees; training/support are stated to be free for program members.
- Documented case of adding a third party's technology: yes: the Turnkey Solutions accreditation process is explicitly designed to validate outside companies' products (e.g., Electrified Motors, named as a "new Turnkey Solutions Partner") for interoperability with Turntide's motors/inverters/software (source: https://turntide.com/community/turntide-technologies-announces-collaboration-with-electrified-motors-as-new-turnkey-solutions-partner/).

- First step: apply through turntide.com/new-partners as an ESCO-tier channel partner, and separately inquire about Turnkey Solutions accreditation for the compressor optimizer.

## Ameresco

- Public pages: https://www.ameresco.com/federal-bid-opportunities/, COMPASS prequalification portal at https://compass.bespokemetrics.com.
- What it offers: Ameresco is an ESCO; it does not run a public technology/measure-supplier channel. Its published intake is a subcontractor/vendor prequalification process (COMPASS SOQ: certifications, org structure, financial risk profile, experience, target-market capabilities, contract history, references), required before bidding on Ameresco projects (typically federal ESPC work).
- Who qualifies: subcontractors/suppliers wanting to bid into specific Ameresco projects, most visibly federal ones (Super ESPC).
- How a third party joins: complete the COMPASS prequalification package at compass.bespokemetrics.com (click "Subcontractor Login"), then Ameresco's opportunity email is proposals@ameresco.com-style contact for active opportunities (page indicates an email contact but full address was not resolved in this pass: verify on the federal-bid-opportunities page directly).
- Published terms: none found regarding revenue share or referral fees; this is a project-by-project subcontracting relationship, not an ongoing measure-supplier program.
- Documented case of adding a third party's hardware/technology: yes, at the subcontractor level: Hannah Solar Government Services (HSGS) delivered as a subcontractor to Ameresco on VA/Army/NASA projects for 5+ years, later formalized into a mentor-protégé relationship.
- Most realistic relationship: none as an ongoing "partner," best framed as project subcontractor: Energy Plus would bid measure-by-measure into specific Ameresco ESPC projects, not sign a standing agreement.
- First step: complete COMPASS prequalification, then monitor Ameresco's federal-bid-opportunities page for projects in South Carolina/Southeast where a compressor-level HVAC measure could be subcontracted in.

## Notes on methodology and gaps

- Several companies (Voltus, CPower, Carrier Abound, Enel X, Sparkfund) publish that partnerships exist but do not publish terms, application forms, or named contacts: this pattern is consistent with enterprise BD-led partnering rather than an absence of any partnering activity.
- Revenue share, referral fee, and exclusivity percentages were "none found" for every company in this list; none of the 24 organizations researched publish specific commercial terms on their public sites. This is expected: such terms are typically disclosed only after an NDA/BD conversation.
- Where a program name appears in Energy Plus's competitive-landscape research (see ../COMPETITIVE-LANDSCAPE-TOP-20.md) as a "direct competitor," this file focuses only on whether that same company also operates a channel a small measure supplier could enter, which is a separate question from competitive overlap.
