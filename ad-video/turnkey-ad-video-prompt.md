# 60-second motion ad: "We handle all of it" (Energy Plus / Dan Knows A Guy)

## Global style (paste at the top of every scene prompt)
Create a premium, high-energy motion-graphics ad. Think Apple keynote reveal meets Stripe / Linear product film: dynamic, kinetic, cinematic, and always crystal clear.

Format: vertical 9:16, 1080x1920, 30fps, 60 seconds total (four 15-second scenes).

Look and feel:
- Kinetic typography is the star. Big, bold, heavy sans-serif type (like Inter Black or Druk) that slams in, scales, slides, splits apart, and reassembles in rhythm with the voiceover. Key words get emphasis through scale jumps, color fills, or a quick glitch or strike-through. Max 5 words on screen at once, readable in under a second.
- Palette: deep navy background (#0A1A33), electric green accent (#3DFF8A) for savings and good news, hot red-orange (#FF4D2E) for waste and cost, and clean white type. Subtle film grain and soft glow.
- Motion language: snappy ease-in-out curves, match cuts, whip pans, morphing shapes (a bill morphs into a chart, a chart morphs into a building), parallax depth, and 3D isometric buildings and rooftop HVAC units. Every cut lands on a beat. Never a static frame for more than 1.5 seconds.
- Data visuals: animated counters, lines drawing themselves, bars collapsing, numbers ticking down. Clean infographic style, no clutter.
- Sound design: driving modern electronic beat around 110 to 120 BPM, with whooshes, clicks, and impact hits synced to the type. A confident, plain-spoken male voiceover (Dan) sits on top.
- Pattern interrupt: the first frame must hook within 1 second.
- No stock-footage feel, no cheesy 3D people, no lens flares overload. Premium and modern.

Recurring motif: a glowing red "waste" layer sitting on a building that gets deleted (dissolved into green particles) as the story resolves.

---

## Scene 1 (0-15s): The problem
- 0-2s: HARD OPEN. A giant energy bill total "$$$" slams onto screen and the number ticks upward fast in red. Impact hit.
- 2-6s: The bill crumples and morphs into an isometric 3D commercial building. Rooftop HVAC units pulse red with heat-shimmer waste. Type punches in word by word: "YOUR BILL" / "KEEPS" / "CLIMBING."
- 6-11s: Eight vendor cards fly in from all sides and stack chaotically around the building: "SOLAR," "BATTERY," "HVAC," "CONTROLS," "FINANCE," "REBATES," "INSTALLER," "TAX." Type: "8 VENDORS." / "8 CONTRACTS."
- 11-15s: The cards jitter and overlap. A red warning stamp hits: "ALL THE RISK." Beat drops out for a split second.
Voiceover: "Your energy bill keeps going up. Fixing it sounds like a capital project, eight vendors, and a lot of risk."

## Scene 2 (15-30s): The guide
- 15-18s: Silence and a single clean beat. All eight cards get sucked into one glowing green point. Type assembles letter by letter: "WE HANDLE" / "ALL OF IT."
- 18-24s: Whip pan to an isometric Arizona car dealership (desert palette, no brand logos). A bill floats above it; the total drops as a counter spins down to "49%" in huge green type. "LOWER BILL" slides in beneath it.
- 24-30s: Small clean text under the number: "Arizona Ford dealership. Client-reported. Results vary." The number pulses once, then shatters into particles that flow into the next scene.
Voiceover: "We take care of all of it. At an Arizona Ford dealership, the bill came down forty-nine percent."

## Scene 3 (30-45s): The plan
Three steps, each with a large animated numeral that draws itself, then its visual:
- 30-35s: "1" draws in green. A meter icon clips onto a rooftop unit; two clean line graphs ("BEFORE" red, "AFTER" green) draw themselves side by side with a "7 DAYS" tag on each. Type: "PROVE IT" / "ON YOUR METER."
- 35-40s: "2" draws in. Icons for equipment, install crew, rebates, and tax credits snap into a neat grid, each checking off with a satisfying click. Type: "WE HANDLE" / "EVERYTHING."
- 40-45s: "3" draws in. A cash-flow line starts above zero and climbs, with a green glow trail. Type: "A PATH TO" / "CASH FLOW" / "DAY ONE."
Voiceover: "We diagnose your building and prove the savings on your own meter. We handle the equipment, the install, and every rebate and tax credit you qualify for. And we find a path to cash flow from day one."

## Scene 4 (45-60s): Call to action and success
- 45-50s: Back to the building from Scene 1. The red waste layer dissolves into green particles; the rooftop units calm down; the bill total ticks down. A single signature line draws itself with a pen stroke. Type: "ONE SIGNATURE."
- 50-55s: Type stacks fast with beat hits: "LOWER BILL." / "NOTHING ELSE" / "ON YOUR PLATE."
- 55-60s: Clean end card on navy. "START THE EXAM" in huge white type, "slashyourenergycost.com" in electric green below, "Dan Knows A Guy" small at the bottom. Final beat hit, then hold for 1.5 seconds.
Voiceover: "One signature. A lower bill. Nothing else on your plate. Start The Exam at slash your energy cost dot com."

## If the tool can only make short clips
Generate each scene as its own 15-second clip with the global style plus that scene's block. Keep the palette, type, and motif identical across clips. Stitch them in order in CapCut, Descript, or Premiere. Lay the voiceover and music as continuous tracks over all four so it plays as one ad, and line the cuts up on the beat.

## If the tool can make 60 seconds in one go
Paste the global style, then all four scenes in order, and ask for one continuous 60-second video with seamless morph transitions between scenes.

## Accuracy rules (keep these)
- "Arizona Ford dealership" only, never the dealership's name. The 49% stays labeled "client-reported."
- Say "a path to cash flow from day one," never "guaranteed."
- Don't use the word "partner."
- Don't add any numbers beyond 49%, 7 days, and 8 vendors.
