What we collect

  • Your answers in the walkthrough: building type, location, approximate bill, and similar details.

Why we use it

  • To prepare your estimate and report, and to send you the copy you asked for.
  • To follow up about your building and, if you agreed, about ways to save energy.
  • To turn your estimate into real numbers when you share usage data and equipment details.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for unrelated advertising.

Where it is stored and who sees it

  • Your details and files are stored in Energy Plus's Google Workspace account, which may keep data on servers in the United States or elsewhere.
  • Utility data you connect is held by Bayou Energy in the United States.
  • The Energy Plus team uses it to prepare your numbers. Our engineers may review it.
  • If we introduce you to a partner, such as a Canadian partner for financing or installation, we share only what that work needs, and we tell you first.

How long we keep it

For as long as we are working with you, and then only as long as needed for our records. Ask us anytime to delete it.

Your choices

  • Stop texts anytime by replying STOP. Stop emails by replying "unsubscribe."
  • Ask to see, correct or delete your information by emailing dan@yourenergyplus.com.
  • Anyone with your private savings plan link can add files to it, so share it only with your team.

  • Your information may be stored and processed in the United States, where it may be accessible to authorities under U.S. law.
  • You can file a complaint with the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada at priv.gc.ca.
